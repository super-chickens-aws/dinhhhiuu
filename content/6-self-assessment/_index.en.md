## Overview

During my participation in the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program organized by **Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam Co., Ltd.**, I had the opportunity to strengthen both my technical knowledge and professional skills through hands-on laboratories, workshops, and the development of a cloud-based application.

Throughout the internship, I gained practical experience with various AWS services, including Amazon Cognito, API Gateway, AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, Amazon S3, CloudFront, Route 53, and Amazon Polly. I also enhanced my software development, system design, documentation, and problem-solving skills while working in a collaborative learning environment.

I consistently completed assigned tasks on schedule, actively explored technical documentation, and communicated with mentors and teammates to overcome challenges. This internship provided valuable real-world experience and significantly improved my confidence in developing cloud-native applications.
