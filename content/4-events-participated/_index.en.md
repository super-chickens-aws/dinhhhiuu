Throughout the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to participate in several technical sharing sessions organized by AWS and its partners. These events not only helped me stay up to date with the latest technology trends but also provided valuable insights into real-world industry practices, career development, and the practical application of AWS services to solve business problems.

---

## Event 1

**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day - June 2026

**Time:** 09:00 AM, June 27, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Participant

### Main Topics

The Community Day focused on current trends in Cloud Computing and Generative AI, featuring presentations from AWS experts and technology partners.

Some of the highlights included:

- Sharing the journey of building cloud-based startups and deploying cloud solutions in enterprises.
- Introducing the architecture and development process of Voice AI systems on AWS.
- Demonstrating a Voice Agent powered by Amazon Bedrock.
- Applying AI to DevOps for automated incident investigation and reducing Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR).
- Using Amazon Q to support Human Resources (HR) operations.
- Security best practices for integrating Amazon Q with third-party systems through MCP Server.

### Key Takeaways

After attending the event, I gained a better understanding of:

- The process of deploying cloud solutions in enterprise environments.
- The challenges of developing Voice AI systems for the Vietnamese language.
- How AI is being applied in DevOps and enterprise management.
- The role of Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Q in building modern AI applications.
- The importance of security when integrating AI models into real-world systems.

## Event 2

**Event Name:** Cloud Architecture & Career Sharing for Swinburne Vietnam Students

**Time:** 09:00 AM, July 4, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Participant

### Main Topics

This seminar brought together experts from AWS, Cloud Kinetics, and Renova Cloud to discuss the future of cloud computing and career development opportunities for students.

The main topics included:

- The Cloud-First strategy adopted by modern enterprises.
- The value of AWS certifications and the practical skills expected by employers.
- Career pathways in Data Engineering.
- How to combine multiple AWS services to solve real business challenges.
- The importance of communication skills, adaptability, and continuous learning in the workplace.

### Key Takeaways

Through this event, I realized that:

- Strong fundamental knowledge is more important than learning individual cloud services in isolation.
- AWS certifications are only the starting point, while practical skills ultimately determine professional success.
- Soft skills, communication, and initiative are just as important as technical expertise.
- Cloud professionals must continuously learn and adapt to keep pace with the rapidly evolving technology landscape.

## Event 3

**Event Name:** FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week

**Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Participant

### Main Topics

This event served as the closing session of the Build Week program, where participating teams presented their Agentic AI solutions developed within a limited timeframe.

Some notable topics included:

- Sharing practical experiences in developing Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) under tight deadlines.
- Presenting Agentic AI architectures integrated with multiple AWS services.
- Applying Computer Vision and Generative AI to solve real-world problems.
- Demonstrating solutions built with AWS Lambda, Amazon Cognito, and AWS Amplify.
- Sharing teamwork strategies, troubleshooting experiences, and time management techniques during hackathons.

### Key Takeaways

After attending the event, I learned several valuable lessons:

- Always identify the real problem before starting product development.
- Focus on delivering a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP) rather than implementing too many features.
- Effective teamwork and clear task allocation significantly improve productivity.
- The ability to troubleshoot and adapt quickly is essential during software development.
- The greatest value of a hackathon lies not in winning prizes but in gaining practical experience, collaborating with teammates, and learning from other participants.
