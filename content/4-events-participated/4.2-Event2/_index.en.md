## Purpose of the Event

The event was organized for Swinburne Vietnam students with the objective of introducing the latest trends in Cloud Computing, sharing practical experiences from industry experts, and providing career guidance for students pursuing careers in Cloud and Data Engineering.

Through real-world stories and professional experiences, the speakers aimed to help students better understand enterprise working environments, the skills required for the industry, and the importance of combining technical knowledge with business thinking to create practical solutions.

---

## Speakers

The event featured experts from AWS and AWS Partners, including:

- AWS
- Cloud Kinetics
- Renova Cloud

The speakers have extensive experience in designing and implementing cloud solutions for enterprise customers and shared valuable insights into both technical expertise and career development.

---

## Key Topics

### Cloud-First Trend

The event began with discussions about the Cloud-First strategy, which has become the preferred approach for organizations worldwide.

Some key highlights included:

- Cloud computing is gradually replacing traditional on-premises infrastructure.
- The cloud market continues to grow rapidly and offers significant career opportunities.
- Businesses are expanding from managing dozens of applications to thousands of cloud-based services.
- Scalability, flexibility, and cost optimization are among the primary reasons organizations are migrating to the cloud.

This session helped participants understand that cloud computing is no longer just a technology trend but has become a fundamental part of enterprise digital transformation.

---

### Career Skills for Cloud Professionals

One of the most valuable sessions focused on the skills required to become a successful Cloud Engineer.

The speakers emphasized that:

- International certifications, such as AWS Certifications, mainly serve as a gateway to pass automated resume screening during recruitment.
- Companies value practical problem-solving skills more than certificates alone.
- Engineers should understand business processes instead of focusing solely on programming.
- Always ask "Why?" before designing a system or implementing a new feature.

This session demonstrated that technical knowledge is only one part of career development, while analytical thinking and the ability to create business value are what truly distinguish outstanding engineers.

---

### Real-World Enterprise Experience

The speakers also shared their personal experiences working in different environments, including startups, technology companies, and large enterprises.

Some valuable lessons included:

- Every organization has different technical requirements and development processes.
- System architects should always consider scalability and long-term reliability when designing solutions.
- Architectural decisions made during the early stages of development significantly impact future system growth.
- Practical experience is often more valuable than theoretical knowledge alone.

These real-world examples gave participants a clearer understanding of the responsibilities and challenges faced by Cloud Engineers in enterprise environments.

---

### Soft Skills and Communication

In addition to technical knowledge, a significant portion of the event focused on professional soft skills.

The speakers discussed:

- Overcoming the fear of making mistakes when starting a new job.
- Effective communication with colleagues and managers.
- The importance of proactively asking questions instead of waiting for guidance.
- Building professional networks (networking).
- Finding career opportunities beyond traditional job-search platforms.

These discussions highlighted that communication skills and initiative are just as important as technical expertise in a professional workplace.

---

### Growth Mindset

The final session provided valuable advice for students preparing to enter the technology industry.

Some memorable messages included:

- Always begin by asking "Why?" to understand the root cause of a problem.
- Never be afraid of making mistakes because failure is an important part of learning.
- Strive to deliver work that exceeds expectations.
- Continuously learn and adapt to keep up with rapidly evolving technologies.

These principles are valuable not only for Cloud Computing but also for long-term professional development.

## Applying the Knowledge

The knowledge gained from this event can be directly applied to the development of my Polly Voice project.

### Designing Scalable Systems

The discussions about cloud architecture reinforced the importance of designing systems with scalability from the beginning.

This supports my decision to adopt a Serverless architecture for Polly Voice, allowing the application to minimize operational costs while remaining easy to scale as the number of users increases.

---

### Combining Technical and Business Perspectives

The event helped me understand that developing an application is not only about implementing technical features but also about solving real user problems.

For Polly Voice, I focused on creating a simple, user-friendly Text-to-Speech workflow that provides practical value for end users.

---

### Developing a Learning Mindset

The advice about continuously asking "Why?" has changed the way I study AWS technologies.

Instead of learning individual services in isolation, I now focus on understanding why each service is chosen within a system architecture and how different AWS services work together to solve real-world business problems.
